## Engineering Philosophy

- **Layered architecture** — controllers handle HTTP, services own business logic, no business logic leaks into routes
- **Observability first** — structured logging, health endpoints, and metrics from day one
- **Configuration over hardcoding** — environment variables, K8s ConfigMaps and Secrets, never credentials in code
- **Document as you build** — Swagger specs, `.env.example` files, and architecture diagrams are part of the deliverable
